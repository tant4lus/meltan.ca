I'm Melissa — an infrastructure and DevOps engineer with a habit of gravitating toward whatever's hardest in the room.

**On the career side**, I've worked as an Infrastructure Engineer, Cloud Engineer, and DevOps Engineer, but the thread running through all of it is the same: bridging development and operations, so developers can get their code out the door without anyone losing sleep over it. That's meant designing CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions and Terraform that give teams fast, automated confidence in build quality, and leading a large-scale migration of retail systems to the cloud across 140+ locations. Somewhere along the way I also picked up a background from UBC.

**Outside of work**, I like putting myself somewhere remote enough that "hard" stops being optional. Backpacking is my summer thing — the biggest trip so far was 10 days through Akshayuk Pass in Auyuittuq National Park, Nunavut, well above the treeline and well past cell service, plus the West Coast Trail on Vancouver Island's Pacific Rim National Park. I'm also a Paddle Canada Level 2 kayaker, with trips through the Broken Group Islands, Haida Gwaii, and the Gulf Islands. In winter I ski, and I'm a CSIA Level 1 instructor — partly because I like skiing, partly because teaching it forces me to actually understand what I'm doing instead of just doing it on instinct.

The biggest trip on record, though, was two months bike touring across Europe on folding bikes, starting in Amsterdam and ending in Gibraltar.

If there's a pattern here, it's that I don't love easy. Whether that's a production system, a mountain pass, or a border crossing on a bike that folds in half, I'd rather find out what I'm capable of than stay comfortable.
